Previous Gen Mods • Welcome back to Los Santos

The way you remember it.

GTA V Gen 7 Restoration is an independent fan-made modification project focused on recreating the seventh-generation GTA V experience on PC. From the original interface and Scaleform elements to classic minimap rendering and pause-menu behavior, the goal is to bring back the details that made the 2013 release distinctive.
