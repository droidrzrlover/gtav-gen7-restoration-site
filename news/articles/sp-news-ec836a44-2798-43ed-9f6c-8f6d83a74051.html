Welcome to the official SharePoint home of the GTA V Gen 7 Restoration Project!!

 

This project focuses on bringing the original Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3 experience of Grand Theft Auto V back to PC, with an emphasis on restoring the original user interface, Scaleform elements, minimap behavior, and other generation-specific features.

What we're working on

Development includes research into the original seventh-generation implementations, recreating classic interface behavior, and improving the accuracy of the restoration.

 

As development progresses, this site will provide project announcements, release information, technical documentation, and installation instructions.
