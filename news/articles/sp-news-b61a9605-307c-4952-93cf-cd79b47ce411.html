November 5, 2026 | Previous Gen Mods | Development News

After extensive development, research, testing, and restoration work, Previous Gen Mods is proud to announce that GTA V Gen 7 Restoration has reached its Version 1.0 development milestone.

The project began with a simple goal: bring the original Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3 experience of Grand Theft Auto V back to PC—not just through visual changes, but by restoring the interface details and behaviors that defined the game's original 2013 release.

More Than a Visual Makeover

GTA V Gen 7 Restoration goes beyond replacing textures or applying graphical presets. The project involves C++ engine-level modifications, Scaleform interface integration, and research into the original seventh-generation implementation.

 

Major restoration areas include:

  • The classic heads-up display and minimap.

  • Original-generation minimap masking and rendering behavior.

  • The pause menu and full-screen map presentation.

  • The classic weapon-selection wheel.

  • Generation-specific interface transitions and presentation details.

 

Every restored component brings the PC experience closer to the original seventh-generation release.

Version 1.0: What's Next?

With the main restoration work reaching its first major milestone, attention is shifting toward release preparation.

 

The remaining work includes confirming compatibility requirements, preparing installation and backup instructions, documenting dependencies, reviewing distribution permissions, and publishing verified release files and checksums.

 

Version 1.0 is not yet available for public download. A release date will be announced once a distributable package is ready.
